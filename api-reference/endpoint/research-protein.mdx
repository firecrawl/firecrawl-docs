---
title: "Look Up Protein"
openapi: "/api-reference/v2-openapi.json GET /search/research/proteins"
---

Look up one protein by UniProt accession, entry name, gene symbol, or exact protein name. The response gives its curated subcellular location, function, and GO cellular-component terms from UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot. Each statement carries its evidence kind and, where UniProt cites one, a `pmid:` paper id that [`GET /search/research/papers/{id}`](/api-reference/endpoint/research-paper) opens.

`match` says how the lookup resolved:

- `found`: one entry matched, and `protein` holds its annotation.
- `ambiguous`: the identifier names more than one entry, or matched only outside the requested organism. `candidates` lists up to 10 entries. Call again with one candidate's `accession`.
- `not_found`: no entry matches. This is a `200`, not a `404`.
