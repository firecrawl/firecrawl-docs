title: "Use Firecrawl MCP in Zoom"

---

Add Firecrawl to Zoom AI Companion and AI Studio custom agents with the Firecrawl app from the Zoom App Marketplace, so your agents can search, scrape, and map the web. You do not need to paste a server URL.

<Note>
Zoom only makes MCP tools available in AI Studio on accounts with Custom AI Companion (CAIC) licenses. Ask your Zoom admin if you are not sure.
</Note>

## Add to Zoom

<Card
  title="Add to Zoom"
  icon="video"
  href="https://marketplace.zoom.us/"
  cta="Open the Zoom App Marketplace"
  arrow
>
  Search for Firecrawl in the Zoom App Marketplace and add the app.
</Card>

1. A Zoom admin opens the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us/), searches for **Firecrawl**, and adds the app.
2. In the Zoom web portal, open **AI Studio**, then **Custom Agents**, then **Create**. In the **Custom agent templates** modal, choose **Start from blank** and complete the required fields.
3. Under **Tools**, choose **Add Tools**, select **Firecrawl**, then **Next**.
4. If prompted, select **Authorize**. When Zoom opens Firecrawl in your browser, sign in, choose a team, and approve access.
5. Select **Confirm** to finish authorization.

Requests through this connection use the credits and plan of the team you selected during sign-in. Review or revoke the connection anytime from [MCP settings](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=mcp).

## Try

With Firecrawl enabled on the agent, try:

```text
Search the web for the latest Firecrawl release notes and summarize the sources.
```

```text
Scrape firecrawl.dev and tell me what it does
```

## Remove

1. In AI Studio, remove Firecrawl from the custom agent's tools, or remove the app in the Zoom App Marketplace.
2. Revoke the connection from [MCP settings](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=mcp). This ends the sign-in grant, and Firecrawl tools stop working until someone authorizes again.

## Troubleshooting

- **Firecrawl is not listed under Add Tools:** ask your Zoom admin to add the Firecrawl app from the Zoom App Marketplace and to confirm the account has CAIC licenses.
- **Tools do not load after you authorize:** revoke the connection in [MCP settings](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=mcp) and authorize again.
- **Still stuck:** email [help@firecrawl.dev](mailto:help@firecrawl.dev).
