Firecrawl is sold by Firecrawl on [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-7k4jqczw7ayo4) and [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/firecrawl-public/firecrawl). Buying this way puts Firecrawl on the cloud bill your team already pays. You get the same hosted API, [SDKs](/sdks/overview), [MCP server](/mcp-server), and [CLI](/sdks/cli) as an account bought on firecrawl.dev.

## Why buy through a cloud marketplace

- **One bill.** Firecrawl charges land on your AWS invoice or your Google Cloud billing account, next to the rest of your cloud spend.
- **Your existing procurement.** You pay with the AWS account or Google Cloud billing account, and the payment method, your team already uses.
- **Terms that fit your volume.** A private offer carries the plan, credit volume, price, and contract length agreed with our team.
- **Google Cloud commitment drawdown.** On Google Cloud, a Marketplace purchase can draw down your Google Cloud spend commitment. See the [FAQ](#faq) below.

## Choose a marketplace

| | AWS Marketplace | Google Cloud Marketplace |

| **How you buy** | Subscribe to the plan listed on AWS Marketplace, or accept a private offer | Private offer only |

## How a private offer works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell us what you need">
    [Contact our sales team](https://www.firecrawl.dev/contact-sales) with your expected volume, concurrency, and security requirements. Include your AWS account ID or your Google Cloud billing account. We scope the plan with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your private offer">
    We send a private offer to your AWS account or Google Cloud billing account with the pricing and terms we agreed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept it in the marketplace">
    Review and accept the offer in the AWS Marketplace console or the Google Cloud console. Charges appear on your cloud bill from then on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    After you accept the offer, our team sets up your Firecrawl account and API key. Set the key as `FIRECRAWL_API_KEY` and follow the [quickstart](/introduction) to make your first request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Firecrawl is a managed service on Google Cloud Marketplace, and Google handles the billing.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same Firecrawl as firecrawl.dev?">
    Yes. Both listings are sold by Firecrawl and give you the same hosted API, SDKs, MCP server, and CLI. On AWS Marketplace, other sellers list packages of the open-source project that run on your own servers. Those listings are not operated or supported by Firecrawl.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is a Marketplace purchase billed?">
    Your cloud provider bills it. On AWS, Firecrawl charges appear on your AWS invoice and are paid with your AWS account's payment method. On Google Cloud, they appear on your Google Cloud bill and are paid through your Google Cloud billing account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy on Google Cloud Marketplace without a private offer?">
    No. The Google Cloud Marketplace listing can only be purchased through a private offer. [Contact our sales team](https://www.firecrawl.dev/contact-sales) to get one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Google Cloud Marketplace purchase count toward my Google Cloud commitment?">
    It can. Google's [commitment drawdown rules](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/commit-drawdown) say Marketplace spend may count toward Minimum Commitment obligations, with exclusions. Google sets which purchases qualify and can change the exclusions, so confirm the terms of your agreement with your Google account team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can a private offer include?">
    A private offer sets your plan, credit volume, price, and contract length, as agreed with our team. On AWS, it can also set the payment schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a private offer?">
    [Contact our sales team](https://www.firecrawl.dev/contact-sales) with your AWS account ID or Google Cloud billing account and what you need. We send the offer to that account, and you accept it in the AWS Marketplace console or the Google Cloud console.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
