With Claude managed access, people in your Claude Team or Enterprise organization can use the [Firecrawl connector in Claude](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/firecrawl) without a separate Firecrawl sign-in. Claude vouches for each person, and Firecrawl connects them to your Firecrawl organization.

This uses Claude's Enterprise Managed Auth with a **Claude-issued** identity source.

## Before you start

- You are an admin or the owner of a Firecrawl organization, and you can open the **Organization** tab in [Settings](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=organization).
- You are an admin of the Claude organization, with access to Claude's managed authorization setup for the Firecrawl connector.

## What you are trusting

While Claude managed access is on, Firecrawl trusts your Claude organization to say who a person is:

- A person whose Claude email matches a member of your Firecrawl organization connects as that member.
- A person whose email has no Firecrawl account gets a new account in your organization's default team, but only when the email's domain is verified for your organization through single sign-on. These accounts are marked in Settings, and you can remove them.
- A person whose email is on any other domain, or already has a Firecrawl account outside your organization, is refused. Invite them to a team in your organization first.

Claude does not tell Firecrawl whether an email address is verified. Register only the issuer of a Claude organization whose members you control.

<Warning>
If your Firecrawl organization requires single sign-on, Claude managed access does not go through your SSO provider. Claude decides who connects.
</Warning>

## Set it up

1. In Claude's managed authorization setup for the Firecrawl connector, choose **Claude-issued** as the identity source and copy the **Issuer URL**. It looks like `https://identity.anthropic.com/issuers/<issuer-id>`.
2. In Firecrawl, open [Settings → Organization](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=organization) and find **Claude managed access**.
3. Paste the Issuer URL, choose the default team for new accounts, and select **Save**.
4. Copy the **Tenant ID** that Firecrawl shows. It starts with `fct_`.
5. Back in Claude's setup, paste the Tenant ID into **Tenant ID** and finish the setup. Firecrawl requires the Tenant ID: without it, sign-ins are refused.

## How connections work

- Each connection uses the credits and plan of the person's team in your organization. A person on several teams in your organization uses their current team, then the default team if they are on it, then the team they joined first.
- Claude receives access that lasts one hour and asks again when it expires. Removing a person or turning off managed access revokes their connections.
- Connections from Claude managed access appear with the person's other connections under [Connected apps](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=mcp).

## Turn it off or change the issuer

- Select **Turn off** in **Claude managed access** to revoke every connection made through it. Select **Turn on** to allow sign-ins again.
- To change the Issuer URL, turn managed access off first, save the new URL, then turn it back on.
- To remove a person that Claude managed access added, select the remove button next to their email. This removes them from every team in your organization.
